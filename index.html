Metrolane Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who we are

Metrolane ("the app", "we") shows live bus positions, plans trips and keeps a record of your rides on your phone. It is an independent app. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with any transport operator.

This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the app has no accounts and no server of ours. What you create in the app stays on your phone. The app does not take payments and does not ask for card or bank details.

What is stored on your phone

The app saves the following in its private storage on your device. None of it is sent to us:

  • Your ride history, trip summaries and details (stops, fare, times, distance).
  • Notifications that the app created for you (reminders and ride alerts).
  • Recent searches, your saved places (Home, Work and favourite stops), and your commute reminders (stop, time and days).
  • The language and appearance (light or dark) you chose, the auto board setting, whether you have seen the welcome pages, and the ride in progress, if any.

Location

The app asks for your precise location only while it is open. It uses it to:

  • find the stop nearest to you;
  • tell whether you are close enough to the bus to start a ride, and start it automatically when you board (if Auto board is on);
  • tell when you have left the bus, so the ride can end by itself, and when you stay on past your stop, so the fare can be updated.

Your location is compared with bus positions on your phone. It is not sent to us, and it is not sent to the live bus service. It is not stored after the app has used it. Outside a ride the app does not use your location in the background. While a ride is active, the Android app keeps running with a visible notification, so alerts and ending the ride when you leave the bus work even with the screen off; this stops when the ride ends.

You can refuse or withdraw the location permission in your phone settings. Searching and live buses still work, but the location features above will not.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows alerts and a ride-in-progress notice on your phone. They are created on your device from your ride and reminders. No notification service of ours is involved. You can turn them off in your phone settings. Commute reminders are scheduled by your phone itself.

Services the app contacts

To work, the app requests data from public services. Like any internet request, these reveal your IP address and basic device information to the service. They do not receive your name, your location or your ride history:

  • Live bus positions, from the public lankametro.lk feed.
  • Map pictures (tiles), from OpenStreetMap. The tile requests show which part of the map you are looking at.
  • Road shapes between stops, from the public OSRM routing service. These requests contain the coordinates of bus stops, not your location.

These services have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them.

Some buttons open other apps: "Open Wallet" opens Samsung Wallet or Google Wallet, and "Email Support" opens your email app with a new message to our support address. Nothing about your rides or fares is passed to them. If you write to us, we receive the email you send, including your email address and anything you put in the message, and we use it only to reply to you. The "Report a problem" button also adds your app version, phone or browser model and language to the email, so we can reproduce the issue; you can read, change or delete that text before you send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • We do not show ads and we do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We do not sell or share your information, and we do not build profiles of you.
  • We do not read your contacts, photos, messages or files.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete a single ride from its receipt, clear your whole history or your notifications from their screens, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data.

Security

The app keeps its data in storage that other apps cannot read, and uses secure (HTTPS) connections to the services above. No method is perfectly secure, so please also protect your phone with a screen lock.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to handle information differently, we will update this policy and the date below before the change takes effect. The current version is always available in the app: Settings, then Privacy Policy.
